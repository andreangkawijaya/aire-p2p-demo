# Payment Terms

## Standard Terms

| Code | Meaning | Days |
|---|---|---|
| NET30 | Net 30 days | 30 |
| NET45 | Net 45 days | 45 |
| NET60 | Net 60 days | 60 |
| 2/10 NET30 | 2% discount if paid within 10 days | 30 |

## Rules

- Default terms for new vendors: NET30
- Early-payment discounts above 1% must be accepted unless cash flow restrictions apply
- Payment runs execute every Tuesday and Friday
