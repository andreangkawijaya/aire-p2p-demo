# Procurement Policy (v2)

> Converted from `raw/procurement-policy-v2.pdf`

## Purchase Approval Thresholds

| Amount (USD) | Required Approval |
|---|---|
| Under $1,000 | Requesting manager |
| $1,000 – $9,999 | Department head |
| $10,000 – $99,999 | Finance controller AND department head |
| $100,000 and above | CFO |

## Key Rules

1. Purchases above $10,000 require dual approval before a purchase order is issued.
2. The three-way match (invoice vs PO vs goods receipt) is mandatory for all payments regardless of amount.
3. Verbal pre-approval is never a reason to skip the match.
