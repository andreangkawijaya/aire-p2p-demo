# Procurement Policy

## Purchase Approval Thresholds

All purchases require approval according to the following authority levels:

| Amount (USD) | Required Approval |
|---|---|
| Under $1,000 | Requesting manager |
| $1,000 – $9,999 | Department head |
| $10,000 – $99,999 | Finance controller AND department head |
| $100,000 and above | CFO |

## Key Rules

1. Purchases above $10,000 require dual approval: the finance controller and the department head must both sign off before a purchase order is issued.
2. No purchase order may be issued without a valid cost center and budget check.
3. Emergency purchases above threshold require retroactive approval within 48 hours.
4. The three-way match (invoice vs PO vs goods receipt) is mandatory for all payments regardless of amount.
